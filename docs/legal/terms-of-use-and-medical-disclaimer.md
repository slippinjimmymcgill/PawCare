## PawCare — Terms of Use (Draft)

*Last updated: [date of actual publication] · Version 0.1 (FYP demo scope)*

### 1. What PawCare Is

PawCare is an academic final-year design project (NUST, Department of Computer Science) providing an AI-assisted pet-health information and record-keeping tool for dog and cat owners. PawCare helps you organize your pet's health information, understand the possible urgency of a symptom, and learn general pet-health information grounded in cited reference sources.

### 2. What PawCare Is Not

**PawCare is not a veterinarian, a veterinary clinic, or a substitute for professional veterinary care.** PawCare does not diagnose medical conditions, does not prescribe medication or dosages, and does not provide treatment plans. Every output PawCare gives you is decision-support information to help you decide *whether and how urgently* to seek a real veterinarian — never a replacement for that visit.

If your pet is showing severe symptoms (difficulty breathing, collapse, seizures, suspected poisoning, uncontrolled bleeding, or similar), **contact a veterinarian or emergency animal hospital immediately.** Do not wait for or rely on PawCare's response in an emergency.

### 3. Accounts & Eligibility

- You must be at least 18 years old (or the age of majority in your jurisdiction) to create a PawCare account, since account creation involves entering personal and pet-health information you're representing as accurate.
- You are responsible for keeping your login credentials secure and for all activity under your account.
- You may delete your assessment/chat history or your account at any time (FR-11); see the Privacy Policy for what that does and doesn't remove.

### 4. Acceptable Use

You agree not to:
- Use PawCare to make real-world treatment decisions without also consulting a licensed veterinarian for anything beyond routine home care.
- Submit another person's pet's health information without authorization to do so.
- Attempt to circumvent, disable, or reverse-engineer the Veterinary Safety Layer's red-flag overrides or output constraints.
- Use PawCare for any species other than dogs and cats — the knowledge base, models, and safety rules are built and validated for companion dogs/cats only (`specs.md` §3.1); PawCare's guidance is not validated for other species and should not be relied on for them.

### 5. AI-Generated Content & Limitations

- PawCare's symptom assessments and assistant responses are generated by AI models grounded in a curated knowledge base, combined with a deterministic Veterinary Safety Layer (FR-08). Despite that grounding, AI-generated content can be incomplete or, rarely, incorrect.
- Confidence indicators and source citations are shown with every assessment and assistant response (FR-05, FR-07) specifically so you can judge how much weight to give any particular answer — treat a low-confidence or uncited claim with proportionate skepticism.
- The Safety Layer is designed to be conservative (over-refer rather than under-refer, per `requirements.md` §4) — if PawCare tells you to see a vet, that recommendation should be followed even if the underlying symptom seems minor to you.

### 6. No Warranty; Limitation of Liability

PawCare is provided "as is," as an academic project, without warranty of any kind, express or implied, including fitness for a particular purpose. To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, the project team, its supervisors, and NUST are not liable for any harm, loss, or damage arising from reliance on PawCare's output in place of professional veterinary care. *(This section, and the governing-law/jurisdiction clause typically paired with it, is exactly the kind of language that needs a qualified-counsel review before any use beyond the FYP demo — flagged, not finalized, here.)*

## Standing Medical Disclaimer (Always-Visible, Point-of-Use)

This is the short-form disclaimer that appears **on every symptom assessment and every assistant response**, matching `safety-rules-v1.yaml`'s `disclaimers.standard` template and FR-08's acceptance criterion that "every response carries the standard 'not a diagnosis' disclaimer":

> **PawCare does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace a veterinarian.** This information is for guidance only. If you're concerned about your pet's health, or symptoms worsen, contact a licensed veterinarian.

Three situational variants, kept **consistent with, not separate from**, `safety-rules-v1.yaml`'s existing `disclaimers` block (this document does not redefine them — it documents where they appear and confirms wording consistency so the two files can't silently drift):

- **Emergency-triage variant** (shown whenever a response resolves to `urgency: emergency`, including every red-flag override): leads with the immediate-action instruction FR-06 requires, before the standard disclaimer — e.g. "This may be a medical emergency. Contact a veterinarian or emergency animal hospital right now." Matches `safety-rules-v1.yaml`'s `disclaimers.emergency` template.
- **Uncertainty variant** (shown when the assessment's confidence is below `safety-rules-v1.yaml`'s `confidence_policy.low_confidence_threshold`): states plainly that PawCare isn't confident in the result and that a vet visit is the reliable next step, rather than presenting a low-confidence guess as if it were solid. Matches `disclaimers.uncertainty`.
- **Toxin-exposure variant** (shown when a red-flag match is in the toxin/poisoning category): adds a pointer to contact a poison-control resource or emergency vet immediately, without naming a specific substance-by-substance treatment (no first-aid dosing instructions — consistent with the Safety Layer's explicit scope decision not to provide step-by-step first aid, documented in `safety-rules-v1.md`). Matches `disclaimers.toxin_exposure`.

**Consistency check performed as part of this task:** read `safety-rules-v1.yaml`'s four disclaimer templates and confirmed this document's standing Terms-of-Use disclaimer (§2 and §5 above) doesn't contradict their wording or scope — e.g., this document doesn't claim any diagnostic capability the YAML's output constraints (`OC-001`–`OC-006`) prohibit the system from actually producing. If `safety-rules-v1.yaml` is version-bumped later (its own governance rule — any edit requires a version bump and fresh review), this document's §2/§5 wording should be re-checked against the new version, not assumed to still match.
